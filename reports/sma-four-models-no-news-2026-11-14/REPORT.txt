WHAT THE COMPARISON DOES AND DOES NOT TEST

The prior with-news version retained zero news features after training preprocessing in the first three folds. Their trained entry weights and results reproduce exactly in this ablation. In the fourth fold, it retained only news_analysis_available and news_feed_available. No article topic, asset sentiment or event-content feature survived in those historical test models.

Thus the changed final-fold result primarily tests removing two availability indicators and the resulting refit, not the predictive value of richer asset-specific news. The no-news run also has a different input dimension, which can change random initialization even with the same seed. This is one deterministic seed per model, not a multi-seed statistical test of news usefulness.

The final with-news models had more recent training data and retained many actual news columns; those are not the models that produced all the historical test trades. They cannot be used to retroactively attribute the historical profit difference to news content.

The same test periods were used as before. They were unseen by each fitted model, but were already examined in earlier research. This remains a retrospective chronological comparison, not a pristine blind test. A later untouched period with adequate contemporaneous news coverage is needed to test whether richer news helps.

CONTROLLED SETTINGS

- Identical frozen market inputs, labels, 84,160 detector setups and 349,094 candidates. Both candidate-array SHA256 hashes match the prior experiment exactly.
- Same eight SMA pairs, one-hour entry window, opposite-cross invalidation, five-minute decisions and strict 24h holding limit.
- Same successful/failed setup definitions, unknown-outcome handling, 48h32m label maturation, 24h purge and all training/validation/test boundaries.
- Same 32/16 networks, seeds, 4,000-update training, regularization, checkpoint grid, threshold grids and independent long/short validation rules.
- Same deterministic exit samples from successful and failed setups. All ten exit networks (two directions across four folds plus the final fit) have identical weights and preprocessing to the prior experiment. Threshold policies may differ because they are reselected on the changed entry model's earlier validation trades.
- News columns are removed, including availability and missingness-related news inputs. Entry vectors contain market, SMA and detector-pair context only: 188 raw features instead of 349. Exit vectors remain 185 raw features.
- Both sides may choose cash. Conflicting long/short entry signals wait; raw scores from independent models are not compared to choose a direction.

RALLY COVERAGE AND CONCENTRATION

Detector coverage is unchanged: 434 of 501 scorable >=+10% bullish / <=-10% bearish rally windows. Trading participation rises from 22 windows with news to 33 without news (6 bullish, 27 bearish). Participation means a same-direction trade within the predefined event window, not capture of its optimum. Windows may overlap and are not independent; their profits are not added separately.

VERIFICATION

All 236 trade payoffs independently match original one-minute candle opens. All 236 runtime entry decisions, runtime exits and complete exit paths reproduce. All four chronological split boundaries pass. No position exceeds 24h, overlaps another position in the same symbol, or reuses the same detector event.

All fold and final artifact input schemas contain zero news fields. A test replaces the news storage with an object that raises on access; building entry inputs still succeeds unchanged. This verifies actual exclusion rather than merely filling news columns with zeros. Target/gain mutation cannot change model inputs. The serialized final inference example passes.

All 46 delivered with-news files remain unchanged, and the original two-stage model's 43 files also remain unchanged. All fixed training/configuration hashes match the pre-fit experiment lock. No test outcome was used to change model settings or repeat selection.
