## Method

Each alert went to a separate ephemeral GPT‑6.1 Sol session at medium reasoning effort. The offline runner requested the model directly. The original and repeat runs used matching hashes for each model input and the common instructions. The model saw the original desk assessment, the confirmed level, closed candles, benchmark moves, timestamped market data and any eligible news. External tools were disabled. The live review labels and subsequent prices were joined after the 27 decisions were recorded.

The original scorer models an entry two minutes after each alert, with a maximum of $100 per case, venue size precision, the historical risk limit, 4.5 basis points in taker fees and 5 basis points of adverse execution per side, and recorded hourly funding. It computes the original observation-deadline exit and an alternate exit at the lead’s stop or target. Candle-modeled P&L depends on the assumed execution prices. This is one decision per model per case.
