TWO SUPERVISED MODELS: ENTRY WITH NEWS, EXIT WITHOUT NEWS
Completed November 14, 2026, Europe/Paris. Offline research artifacts.

Request and implemented target
Scan every recorded native token for rolling24h price gains >=10%. The market
database stores historical prices, so the percentage is calculated from same-
venue five-minute closing observations exactly24h apart. Repeated qualifying
readings are grouped with one anchor per token per24h. No SMA crossover is
required for target discovery or live entry; SMA measurements remain features.

For each anchor, the teacher searches entries in the preceding24h through the
anchor and exits5m to24h AFTER each entry. It chooses the maximum executable
gross-return pair. An entry target can be one5m bar away if it preserves >=95%
of that optimum. The exit model trains on exactly these same positive entry
points, each with its own optimal exit. Non-target eligible periods are entry
negatives; incomplete/censored teacher searches are excluded, not mislabeled.
The >=10% screen is a future training label, never a live-entry gate.

Execution
Both decisions use five-minute completed bars. The decision arrives5 seconds
after close; a hypothetical fill uses the following minute open55 seconds later.
The maximum holding period is24h from the actual entry fill. The exit is chosen
every5m; only its upper bound is fixed. A missing terminal open may retry for
up to30m, recorded separately. Entry uses100 USD and requires at least10,000 USD
approximate quote volume in the preceding minute. Fees, funding payments and
slippage are zero. One position per symbol; no positions bridge archive gaps.
Reported P&L sums per-symbol100 USD trades, not a shared-capital account ROI.

Dataset
235 native token symbols; 29,979 qualifying five-minute readings.
355 usable target rallies across 122 tokens; 354 unique exact optimal entry points before the near-optimal tolerance.
103,281 mature eligible classification states, including 430 positive entry targets.
Excluded rally searches: {'no_liquid_executable_teacher_entry': 97, 'unfinished_rally_teacher_horizon': 16, 'unresolved_teacher_search_terminal': 14, 'no_positive_executable_teacher_gain': 1}.
3,221,970 global five-minute states; 1,993,884 spot candles; 1,477,653 derivative snapshots; 10,614 usable book snapshots.
11,787 account-scoped news documents. Earliest news availability: 2026-10-31T09:46:30.819Z.
News unavailable before the feed existed is missing, not neutral sentiment.
Book coverage is sparse; some inputs disappear under train-only preprocessing.
Whale-source news is reporting, not a verified wallet or exchange-flow ledger.

Independent models
Entry: 137 market inputs plus 56 news inputs. Final fitting used 87,470 states and 400 positive targets; 71 positive targets had news-feed coverage.
Exit: 137 market inputs plus 8 trade-state inputs. It trained on 400 of the same positive entry points. There are zero news inputs.
The exit sees entry-relative return, elapsed/remaining holding time, running
gain/drawdown/rebound since entry, and entry price. No entry-model score or
news-trained hidden representation is passed into it.
Both networks have hidden layers64/32 and run4,000 class-balanced BCE updates.
Validation retained entry checkpoint 200 and exit checkpoint 1500.
Scores are classifier scores, not calibrated probabilities of a profitable trade.

News-specific qualification
The older fitted folds had no news-era observations in their training sets.
Only 1 test fold(s) used a model trained with retained news features.
That subset produced -43.68 USD over 3 trades. The overall result is not evidence that news improved the strategy.

Exit diagnosis with predefined good entries
On 185 held-out target-entry examples, the learned exit averaged 9.76% versus a hindsight best 17.20%.
Aggregate capture for these same entry points: 56.75%.
Fixed hindsight-good entries supplied to the exit model. Overlapping teacher positions are independent diagnostics; this is not an executable portfolio or end-to-end performance.
Nearby positive entry points can belong to the same rally, so these are not all
independent rallies. This diagnostic does not imply the entry model can find them.

Selection and limits
Entry checkpoints use validation average precision. Exit checkpoints/thresholds
use return at fixed teacher validation entries. End-to-end validation chooses
the entry threshold with cash available. All source/label timestamps and model
selection precede the outer test. Labels mature48h32m after a decision; an
additional24h purge separates training labels from validation and test windows.
Validation starts with the latest two eligible dates and expands by dates until
at least five positive targets exist. Sparse positives make this selection noisy.

An initial replay assertion found an archive-boundary mistake; it was repaired
before delivery and all fits were rerun. A coverage check also found the initial
20%-of-history validation split left no news in the outer training sets. The
shorter coverage-based split above replaced it. Preliminary outcomes had been
viewed. Those runs are preserved; changes are recorded in the experiment lock.
These dates were already examined in earlier research and the SOPH example was
previously inspected. They are unseen to each fitted model, not pristine research
holdouts. The changed all-token universe prevents direct dollar comparisons with
the older SMA-gated study. This is not a validated dependable trading edge.

Saved final policy
Latest validation chose cash: True. Entry threshold: None; exit threshold: 0.25.
Both trained models are saved even when validation chooses to abstain. The final
models have no later prospective test in this archive.

Verification
Passed: 331 trade-payoff checks, 24 original-minute source checks, 24 independent as-of news counts, 185 teacher exit bounds, and 152 saved-model action calls.
Independent full oracle searches checked on 30 target rallies. Both models pass numerical gradient checks.
No executed-position overlap; no news columns accepted by the exit API. Source
timestamps are checked, but exact historical publication/network latency and
later exchange corrections are not independently established.
